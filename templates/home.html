About This Project

Even though the HIV pandemic has been around for decades now, it's disheartening to see misinformation still causing real harm in the communities. HealthQuest was inspired by my desire to contribute to HIV education and awareness by creating a platform that made learning about HIV both engaging and accessible to everyone. I believe that staying informed is a powerful act of self-care and essential for positive living.

This project began as part of my journey in Holberton School's software engineering program, where I had the opportunity to turn this idea into a reality as part of my portfolio work. The project kicked off in August 2024 when I started researching various educational tools, realizing that quizzes could make learning more interactive. After weeks of coding, database design, and research, HealthQuest came to life!
